## v1.0.0 (2026-10-03)

First release.

- Color palette sampled from Artemis II photographs of the Moon and Earth.
- Omarchy shell surfaces: bar, menus, launcher, notifications, polkit prompt,
  lock screen and image picker.
- Earthshine: a soft Earth-blue glow on the focused window, with borders
  fading from Earth blue to lunar highlight.
- btop theme with lunar gradients.
- Six Artemis II backgrounds and two generated pixel wordmark wallpapers; the
  Lunar Quest wordmark is the default background.
- Plymouth and SDDM unlock mark.
- Theme picker and unlock-screen previews.
